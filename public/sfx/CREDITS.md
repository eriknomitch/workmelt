# Sound credits

Generated by `tools/encode-sfx.mjs`. Everything in `public/sfx/` is derived
from the packs below — trimmed, normalized, downmixed to mono and encoded to
48 kHz Opus. Sources live in `assets-src/` (gitignored, `tools/fetch-sfx.sh`).

## The Free Firearm Sound Library

- **Author:** Ben Jaszczak, Brian Nelson, Kevin Heras, Matthew Nanney
- **License:** CC0 1.0 (public domain)
- **Source:** https://opengameart.org/content/the-free-firearm-sound-library
- **Used for:** all weapon shots

## Footsteps on different surfaces

- **Author:** congusbongus
- **License:** CC BY 3.0 — **attribution required**
- **Source:** https://opengameart.org/content/footsteps-on-different-surfaces
- **Used for:** footsteps: concrete, plaster, metal, wood, dirt, foliage, water

Anything without a sample here is synthesized at runtime by
